# DuitGwe

DuitGwe is simply a money recording apps or something, idk how you call it. All files will be saved locally.

On the front, the "Home" page, there will be display that says "Tunai" and "Non-Tunai" at the top of the screen, it shows you the money you had in apps has recorded both cash or non-cash.
Below it you will see "Dompet Saya" and some "+" icon. On there you can add your kind of wallet, i.e. i have "Wallet" and "Bank" in my acc.
You can add your new wallet and choose whether it was cash or non-cash. You can also edit it afterward with holding any wallet down, so whatever.

Below it you will see 3 button, "Pemasukan", "Pengeluaran", and "Transfer".
Pemasukan: basically add amount of money of you choosen wallet and category (i'll explain this later), you can input the amount and the description of what purpose does record do.
Pengeluaran: same thing with Pemasukan, but the opposite, it just reduce the amount of money.
Transfer: this one is kinda useless unless you are me. this one basically transfer the money from one wallet to your another wallet without having to add and reduce the amount from 2 wallet manually.

Below those three, you will see "+ Atur Kategori", this one is just adding category of the transaction. You CAN'T edit it afterward so keep in mind.

And what purpose this category serve? the answers lies on the next page, the "Histori" page. 
It is a history of transaction you've been made monthly. There is a filter for the list displayed with the "Atur Filter" button.
It will show you the money in (Pemasukan) and out (Pengeluaran) in a month, there is also the difference of those two below it. 
And lastly, there will be the list of the transaction monthly. You can see the description, category, wallet, amount of money, and date of the transaction been made.
If you somehow misinput the amount or anything, you can delete it here with holding the transaction down. Don't worry, the money would be given back as it was.

That's it, this app is purely for my own need, so i might won't change it in the future unless i need to, but feel free to use it (tho it is a bad one).
